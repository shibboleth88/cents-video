CENTS & digital objecthood — RECORDING SCRIPT
====================================================

24 sentences, 196 words.
At 130-145 words a minute that is about 81-90 seconds of speech, before pauses.

HOW TO RECORD
  One take, straight through. Do not stop to fix a stumble — say the line
  again and carry on; the alignment keeps whatever you actually said, and
  a second attempt inside one take is easier to cut than a second take.
  Leave a breath between sentences. Leave four seconds of silence at the
  end: the credits live there.
  Changing a line as you read is fine. The transcript follows your voice,
  not the page, and anything you add is flagged for review rather than
  overwritten.

  Save as: inputs/vo_raw.wav

----------------------------------------------------

 1.  A copper penny is worth one cent.

 2.  The copper inside it is worth about two and a half.

 3.  Rutherford Chang's CENTS begins in that gap.
      [CENTS: say 'cents' — the PROJECT (capitals are for the reader, not the voice)]
      [Rutherford: RUTH-er-ford]

 4.  Over several years he sorted a hundred thousand pennies down to ten thousand pre-1982 coppers.
      [1982: 'nineteen eighty-two']
      [pre-1982: 'pre nineteen eighty-two']

 5.  He photographed and catalogued every one.

 6.  Then he melted them into a single block.

 7.  Then he inscribed each destroyed penny onto a sat, the smallest divisible unit of Bitcoin.
      [sat: rhymes with 'cat'; a satoshi]

 8.  The block itself filled an entire Bitcoin block — CENTS Block 839969.
      [839969: read as 'eight three nine, nine six nine']

 9.  He released all ten thousand at one sat each.

10.  Each Cent is five natures at once.
      [Cent: one collection piece, said the same as 'cent' — the script uses the singular too]

11.  A unit of currency.

12.  A quantity of copper.

13.  A melted element of a sculpture.

14.  A photograph.

15.  A Bitcoin inscription.

16.  All of these things, and none of them.

17.  Conceptual art broke from the object in the nineteen sixties, when commodification had hollowed it out.

18.  Digital flows could not hold identity, singularity, provenance or ownership.

19.  They lacked objecthood.
      [objecthood: OB-ject-hood]

20.  Blockchain gave them discrete boundaries and verifiable histories.

21.  Blockchain is this era's objecthood.

22.  CENTS holds that whole arc in one gesture.

23.  Its medium is the gesture of its continual becoming.

24.  A digital object is simultaneously itself, not itself, and an interpretation of itself.

----------------------------------------------------

AFTER RECORDING

  pipeline/audio_clean.sh inputs/vo_raw.wav public/vo.wav
  python3 pipeline/align.py public/vo.wav --script script/voiceover.txt
  npm run render && npm run render:captioned && npm run render:essential
  python3 pipeline/verify.py

  The edit re-cuts itself around your timing. Nothing needs re-timing by hand.
