CENTS & digital objecthood — RECORDING SCRIPT
====================================================

24 sentences, 198 words.
At 130-145 words a minute that is about 82-91 seconds of speech, before pauses.

HOW TO RECORD
  One take, straight through. Do not stop to fix a stumble — say the line
  again and carry on; the alignment keeps whatever you actually said, and
  a second attempt inside one take is easier to cut than a second take.
  Leave a breath between sentences. Leave four seconds of silence at the
  end: the credits live there.
  Changing a line as you read is fine. The transcript follows your voice,
  not the page, and anything you add is flagged for review rather than
  overwritten.

  Save as: inputs/vo_raw.wav

----------------------------------------------------

 1.  Brenner's Lincoln, on the penny, is the most reproduced artwork on Earth.

 2.  This is the readymade Rutherford Chang chose.
      [Rutherford: RUTH-er-ford]

 3.  He brings a black hole humor to it — laughter that resonates within emptiness.

 4.  It is absurd that a nation's currency should dissolve into worthlessness.

 5.  It is absurd that a cent should be valued at hundreds or thousands of dollars.

 6.  Yet, here we are.

      [Cent: one collection piece, said the same as 'cent' — the script uses the singular too]

 9.  In circulation, value defined by the Treasury.

10.  Removed from circulation, that assignment subverted.

11.  Archivally photographed, valued for what the image holds.

12.  Destroyed, its value made undefinable.

13.  Inscribed on Bitcoin, continually redefined by decentralized consensus.
      [decentralized: four syllables, do not rush]

14.  Commodification hollowed out the art object.

15.  A split opened between materiality and meaning.

16.  Digitization widened it.

17.  Digital flows could not hold identity, singularity, provenance or ownership.

18.  They lacked objecthood.
      [objecthood: OB-ject-hood]

19.  Blockchain gave them discrete boundaries and verifiable histories.

20.  Blockchain is this era's objecthood.

21.  CENTS holds that arc in one gesture.
      [CENTS: say 'cents' — the PROJECT (capitals are for the reader, not the voice)]

22.  Its medium is the gesture of its continual becoming.

23.  Digital objects on blockchain are a new form of conceptual art.

24.  A thing is simultaneously itself, not itself, and an interpretation of itself.
