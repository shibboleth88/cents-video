Brenner's Lincoln, on the penny, is the most reproduced artwork on Earth.
This is the readymade Rutherford Chang chose.
He brings a black hole humor to it — laughter that resonates within emptiness.
It is absurd that a nation's currency should dissolve into worthlessness.
It is absurd that a cent should be valued at hundreds or thousands of dollars.
Yet, here we are.
The object remained, buoyed into physicality by the force of its context.
Every Cent passes through five transformations of value.
In circulation, value defined by the Treasury.
Removed from circulation, that assignment subverted.
Archivally photographed, valued for what the image holds.
Destroyed, its value made undefinable.
Inscribed on Bitcoin, continually redefined by decentralized consensus.
Commodification hollowed out the art object.
A split opened between materiality and meaning.
Digitization widened it.
Digital flows could not hold identity, singularity, provenance or ownership.
They lacked objecthood.
Blockchain gave them discrete boundaries and verifiable histories.
Blockchain is this era's objecthood.
CENTS holds that arc in one gesture.
Its medium is the gesture of its continual becoming.
Digital objects on blockchain are a new form of conceptual art.
A thing is simultaneously itself, not itself, and an interpretation of itself.
