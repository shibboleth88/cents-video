What happens when a systems artwork produces something that the system itself
cannot witness? Rutherford Chang's CENTS begins with a discrepancy. Each piece
starts as an ordinary penny, which is then photographed, catalogued and
inscribed onto a satoshi. Three artefacts result, and they are not the same
artefact: the physical penny, the photographic record, and the inscription that
carries the image on chain.

The object persists. The record of the object persists. What goes missing is the
link between them — the correspondence that made the three one work. That gap is
not an error in the work. It is the work's newest layer, produced by the
infrastructure rather than authored by the artist.

Recovery is not a matter of attention alone. It requires control of the output,
recognition of which satoshi carries the inscription, and a transaction that
separates that satoshi from the rest. Inscription-aware handling can do this.
Ordinary handling will not, because ordinary handling has no reason to look.
