This is the Wall of ScrappyT.
Its 41 Cents hold every one of the nineteen traits in the Traits Atlas.
As of October 2026, no other Wall on cents.vision does. Even the artist wallet, with a thousand Cents, has no Iridescent Cent.
Its signature Cent, number 7139, from 1967, carries two of them: a full desert patina, and Ghost.
Ghost means a full or near-full patina sets Lincoln apart from the field. Of the thirty-three Ghost Cents, only thirteen carry a full desert patina.
Here are the other seventeen, one Cent each.
Verdigris, green to teal: number 8550, significant.
Obsidian, dark brown to black: number 8394, significant.
Red, scarlet to russet: number 9258, full.
Notable means clearly visible but local, significant a major presence on the face, and full means the patina dominates the coin.
Gold, honey gold to pale amber: number 5856.
Orange, given only to clear standouts: number 9469.
Iridescent, a copper surface overlaid with gold, violet and blue: number 7713. Only twenty Cents carry it, and this Wall holds two.
Corroded, oxidation that has gone into the metal: number 366.
Lustrous, original mint shine: number 830.
Striated, parallel lines made by metal flow during striking: number 1295.
Abraded, copper missing from gouges: number 8136, heavily abraded.
Faceless, Lincoln's features lost: number 1965.
Fingerprint, ridges clear enough to read at once: number 508.
White drips and streaks, pale runs across the patina: number 2654.
Contaminated, foreign matter added after minting: four of the twelve types, special oddities, obscure adhesive, speckles and alien secretion.
Assassin, one round mark on Lincoln's head, neck or body: number 739.
Vandalized, marks that show deliberate human intent: number 7745. Only twenty-six Cents carry it.
And Wheat, struck 1958 or earlier, with wheat ears on the back: number 3824, from 1956, the earliest year on the Wall.
Nineteen of nineteen.
The Wall spans two wallets and the years 1956 to 1982, which puts it in the Quarter Club, for twenty-five to forty-nine Cents.
It completes six Sets, among them a Spectrum Set: all four base patinas, plus gold and orange. As of October 2026, ScrappyT is one of only fifteen collectors to hold one.
The Wall carries Diamond Hands: it has held its position for at least a year, with little to no selling.
And it is a Mint Keeper: ScrappyT minted four of its Cents and still holds them, never sold.
