This is the Wall of ScrappyT.
Its signature Cent, number 7139, was struck in 1967, carries a full desert patina, and is a Ghost Cent.
Ghost means a full or near-full patina sets Lincoln apart from the field. Of the thirty-three Ghost Cents, only thirteen carry a full desert patina.
The Wall holds 41 Cents in two wallets, from 1956 to 1982, which puts it in the Quarter Club, for twenty-five to forty-nine Cents.
One of them is a wheat penny, minted 1958 or earlier, with two ears of wheat on the back. It is from 1956, the earliest year on the Wall.
It holds three Cents from 1971, for the 1971 Club.
Two of its Cents carry a full patina: the full desert and a full red.
Two are Iridescent, a copper surface overlaid with gold, violet and blue. And two are Ghost Cents.
The Wall completes six Sets.
A Spectrum Set: a significant verdigris, a significant obsidian, a full red, a full desert, a notable gold and an orange.
Notable means clearly visible but local, significant a major presence on the face, and full means the patina dominates the coin.
As of October 2026, ScrappyT is one of only fifteen collectors to hold a Spectrum Set.
A Chronicle Set: a Cent from 1971, from 1979 and from 1982.
A Full Mint Set: an S mint, a D mint and a Cent with no mint mark.
A Contamination Set, four of the twelve contamination types: special oddities, obscure adhesive, speckles and alien secretion.
And a 1970s Set and a 1980s Set: every mint year from 1970 to 1982.
The Wall carries Diamond Hands: it has held its position for at least a year, with little to no selling.
And it is a Mint Keeper: ScrappyT minted four of its Cents and still holds them, never sold.
