[thoughtfully] [calm] This is the Wall of Scrappy T.
[thoughtfully] [calm] Its signature Cent, number seventy-one thirty-nine, was struck in nineteen sixty-seven, carries a full desert patina, and is a Ghost Cent.
[thoughtfully] [calm] Ghost means a full or near-full patina sets Lincoln apart from the field. Of the thirty-three Ghost Cents, only thirteen carry a full desert patina.
[thoughtfully] [calm] The Wall holds forty-one Cents in two wallets, from nineteen fifty-six to nineteen eighty-two, which puts it in the Quarter Club, for twenty-five to forty-nine Cents.
[thoughtfully] [calm] One of them is a wheat penny, minted nineteen fifty-eight or earlier, with two ears of wheat on the back. It is from nineteen fifty-six, the earliest year on the Wall.
[thoughtfully] [calm] It holds three Cents from nineteen seventy-one, for the nineteen seventy-one Club.
[thoughtfully] [calm] Two of its Cents carry a full patina: the full desert and a full red.
[thoughtfully] [calm] Two are Iridescent, a copper surface overlaid with gold, violet and blue. And two are Ghost Cents.
[thoughtfully] [calm] The Wall completes six Sets.
[thoughtfully] [calm] A Spectrum Set: a significant verdigris, a significant obsidian, a full red, a full desert, a notable gold and an orange.
[thoughtfully] [calm] Notable means clearly visible but local, significant a major presence on the face, and full means the patina dominates the coin.
[thoughtfully] [calm] As of October twenty twenty-six, Scrappy T is one of only fifteen collectors to hold a Spectrum Set.
[thoughtfully] [calm] A Chronicle Set: a Cent from nineteen seventy-one, from nineteen seventy-nine and from nineteen eighty-two.
[thoughtfully] [calm] A Full Mint Set: an S mint, a D mint and a Cent with no mint mark.
[thoughtfully] [calm] A Contamination Set, four of the twelve contamination types: special oddities, obscure adhesive, speckles and alien secretion.
[thoughtfully] [calm] And a nineteen seventies Set and a nineteen eighties Set: every mint year from nineteen seventy to nineteen eighty-two.
[thoughtfully] [calm] The Wall carries Diamond Hands: it has held its position for at least a year, with little to no selling.
[thoughtfully] [calm] And it is a Mint Keeper: Scrappy T minted four of its Cents and still holds them, never sold.
