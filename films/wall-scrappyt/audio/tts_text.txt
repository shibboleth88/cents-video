This is the Wall of Scrappy T.
Its forty-one Cents hold every one of the nineteen traits in the Traits Atlas.
As of October twenty twenty-six, no other Wall on cents dot vision does. Even the artist wallet, with a thousand Cents, has no Iridescent Cent.
Its signature Cent, number seventy-one thirty-nine, from nineteen sixty-seven, carries two of them: a full desert patina, and Ghost.
Ghost means a full or near-full patina sets Lincoln apart from the field. Of the thirty-three Ghost Cents, only thirteen carry a full desert patina.
Here are the other seventeen, one Cent each.
Verdigris, green to teal: number eighty-five fifty, significant.
Obsidian, dark brown to black: number eighty-three ninety-four, significant.
Red, scarlet to russet: number ninety-two fifty-eight, full.
Notable means clearly visible but local, significant a major presence on the face, and full means the patina dominates the coin.
Gold, honey gold to pale amber: number fifty-eight fifty-six.
Orange, given only to clear standouts: number ninety-four sixty-nine.
Iridescent, a copper surface overlaid with gold, violet and blue: number seventy-seven thirteen. Only twenty Cents carry it, and this Wall holds two.
Corroded, oxidation that has gone into the metal: number three sixty-six.
Lustrous, original mint shine: number eight thirty.
Striated, parallel lines made by metal flow during striking: number twelve ninety-five.
Abraded, copper missing from gouges: number eighty-one thirty-six, heavily abraded.
Faceless, Lincoln's features lost: number nineteen sixty-five.
Fingerprint, ridges clear enough to read at once: number five oh eight.
White drips and streaks, pale runs across the patina: number twenty-six fifty-four.
Contaminated, foreign matter added after minting: four of the twelve types, special oddities, obscure adhesive, speckles and alien secretion.
Assassin, one round mark on Lincoln's head, neck or body: number seven thirty-nine.
Vandalized, marks that show deliberate human intent: number seventy-seven forty-five. Only twenty-six Cents carry it.
And Wheat, struck nineteen fifty-eight or earlier, with wheat ears on the back: number thirty-eight twenty-four, from nineteen fifty-six, the earliest year on the Wall.
Nineteen of nineteen.
The Wall spans two wallets and the years nineteen fifty-six to nineteen eighty-two, which puts it in the Quarter Club, for twenty-five to forty-nine Cents.
It completes six Sets, among them a Spectrum Set: all four base patinas, plus gold and orange. As of October twenty twenty-six, Scrappy T is one of only fifteen collectors to hold one.
The Wall carries Diamond Hands: it has held its position for at least a year, with little to no selling.
And it is a Mint Keeper: Scrappy T minted four of its Cents and still holds them, never sold.
