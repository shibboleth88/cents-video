This is the Wall of DOPEMIND, newly claimed on cents.vision.
Its signature Cent is number 478, from 1963.
In the Numismatists Discord, DOPEMIND asked: "What should I add to expand the set? Wondering about the direction for this collection."
As of November 2026, the Wall holds 12 Cents, from 1963 to 1981.
Eight carry a patina: verdigris, obsidian or desert.
What it does not hold: a red patina, a wheat penny, or a Cent from 1969 or 1971.
centoshi replied: "You already know my answer." DOPEMIND wrote back: "1971."
centoshi holds 16 Cents from 1971, more than any other collector.
Of 1971, Martin Lehnen wrote: "It was the year the United States abandoned the gold standard, severing the connection between currency and tangible value."
A 1971 Cent would add the 1971 Club badge. With the Wall's 1979, it would leave a Chronicle Set one 1982 short.
Daidatep suggested six Cents, and added: "I could go on and on."
Number 8657 is from 1965. Only 90 Cents are from 1965, fewer than any other year of the 1960s.
Number 8820, from 1972, carries red patina. With all four base patinas, the card would add a Patina Set.
Number 6451 is from 1980. With the Wall's 1981, it would leave a 1980s Set one 1982 short.
Number 6875, from 1967, is heavily abraded, one of 44. In the Traits Atlas, heavy abrasion "dominates the read of the coin."
Number 6782, a 1969 from Denver, would add the 1969 Club badge.
And number 5888, a 1956 from Denver, would be the Wall's first wheat penny.
