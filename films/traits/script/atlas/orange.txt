 CENTS Traits Lab: Traits Atlas – Orange Patina ← Traits Atlas Index Traits Atlas Codebook, Minimums, Exceptions Verdigris Obsidian Red Patina Orange Patina Orange Patina Summary: A warm orange surface phase that sits between raw copper brightness and deeper reds. It reads as luminous and energetic. Color: Tangerine, copper-orange, and amber/ochre; highlights can stay bright while open fields shift toward uniform orange with subtle interference. Recesses may deepen toward sienna. Chemistry: Typically thin cuprite (Cu 2 O) films over metallic copper producing orange hues by optical effects; trace oxides/carbonates can push the tone toward amber or sienna. Often a transitional stage before redder patinas. Classification: Orange is a sharpened trait category. We don’t use a „notable” and „full” designation for Orange; instead, the trait is reserved for clear standout examples that present a signature orange read. Borderline or orange-leaning Cents are intentionally excluded. Continuity: The orange effect should form continuous regions or a unified film logic (not scattered “islands” or mixed-field ambiguity). Exclusion of overlaps: If the coin reads primarily as Red, Gold/Desert, or “orange-leaning” within another patina, it is left out of Orange. Version 1.1
