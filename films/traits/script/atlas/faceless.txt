The portrait reads as erased rather than merely toned or scratched–an absence that overtakes the coin’s primary figure. Definition: Faceless requires an overwhelming loss of facial features (eyes, nose, mouth, cheek modeling). If the outline of Lincoln’s face and head is still clearly readable across most of the portrait, the coin should not be classified as Faceless. Visual Character: Flattened relief with minimal feature cues; fields and devices may merge. Causes can vary (abrasion, corrosion, contamination, or deliberate alteration), but the outcome is a dominant visual erasure.
