# Traits film — plan, draft 1

One film covering every trait the Trait Council named (user, 2026-10-24: "one
film around 5 minutes... define notable, significant, full once... if 5
minutes is too short, perhaps it can be a longer film").

Supersedes the series recommendation in treatment v0.1
(https://claude.ai/artifact/TzARVDwYEQLgbCZrQgdZJp), which the user declined.

## Sources

- Definitions: the Council's Traits Atlas, cents.vision/traits-atlas.html and
  /traits-atlas/<trait>.html, fetched 2026-10-24, saved as text in
  `script/atlas/`. Narration paraphrases these; no line adds a cause the atlas
  does not give.
- Counts: Sep 2026 trait export,
  `/mnt/project-files/knowledge/data/cents_by_trait.json`.

## Runtime (estimate, not measured)

Draft 2 (user, 2026-10-24: short intro on the cataloguing process only,
matter-of-fact throughout, end without fanfare on the untraited half) is 48
lines, about 495 words. At the eo pace measured on Sets film A (take B: 365
words in 142.1 s) narration alone is about 3:15. With every trait beat held at
least 8 s (hero 4 s + wall 4 s) the film estimates at about **5:10**, before
pans on the larger walls. Measure after the voice take.

Draft 1 (`script/voiceover_draft1.txt`, 599 words, est. 5:53) is kept: its
intro restated the project and its close was rhetorical; both were cut.

## Every beat: hero, then wall

Hero = the atlas's own example Cent where it names one, 1050 px front,
slow bounded turn. Wall = every Cent when 48 or fewer (6 x 8 at ~170 px is the
smallest readable grid), otherwise a labelled sample ("48 of 1,493").
