This is the Wall of Dope Mind.
Its signature Cent, number four seventy-eight, was struck in nineteen sixty-three. It carries a significant obsidian patina, a notable desert patina, and Speckles, small particles scattered across the face.
Notable means clearly visible but local, and significant a major presence on the face.
Speckles is one of the twelve types of contamination, foreign matter added after minting.
Number eighty-seven forty-one, from nineteen seventy-nine, one of the three Chronicle Set years, carries two more types at once: paint, a colored layer, and obscure adhesive, thick, cloudy deposits.
Only thirteen of the ten thousand Cents carry two types of contamination.
Together, these two Cents cover three of the twelve types, which makes a Contamination Set.
As of November twenty twenty-six, fifty-eight collectors hold one. Dope Mind holds only two contaminated Cents, the fewest of any Wall with this Set.
The Wall holds twelve Cents, from nineteen sixty-three to nineteen eighty-one, which puts it in the Dime Club, for ten to twenty-four Cents.
Two of them carry a D mint mark, for Denver, from nineteen sixty-three and nineteen seventy-four. The Wall holds both years without a mint mark as well.
Number thirty-three ninety-six, from nineteen seventy-six, carries white drips and streaks, pale runs across the patina. Eighty-two Cents carry them.
And the Wall carries Diamond Hands: it has held its position for at least a year, with little to no selling.
