This is the Wall of DOPEMIND.
Its signature Cent, number 478, was struck in 1963. It carries a significant obsidian patina, a notable desert patina, and Speckles, small particles scattered across the face.
Notable means clearly visible but local, and significant a major presence on the face.
Speckles is one of the twelve types of contamination, foreign matter added after minting.
Ninety-five Cents carry Speckles. Obsidian is on fewer than one in five Cents, but on seventy of these ninety-five.
Only five pair Speckles with significant obsidian, and number 478 is the only one of them with desert as well.
No other Cent of the 10,000 carries all three.
Number 8741, from 1979, one of the three Chronicle Set years, carries two more types at once: paint, a colored layer, and obscure adhesive, thick, cloudy deposits.
Together, these two Cents cover three of the twelve types, which makes a Contamination Set.
As of November 2026, fifty-eight collectors hold one. DOPEMIND holds only two contaminated Cents, the fewest of any Wall with this Set.
Only thirteen of the 10,000 Cents carry two types of contamination at once, and number 8741 is one of them.
The Wall holds twelve Cents, from 1963 to 1981, which puts it in the Dime Club, for ten to twenty-four Cents.
Two of them carry a D mint mark, for Denver, from 1963 and 1974. The Wall holds both years without a mint mark as well.
Number 3396, from 1976, carries white drips and streaks, pale runs across the patina. Eighty-two Cents carry them.
And the Wall carries Diamond Hands: it has held its position for at least a year, with little to no selling.
DOPEMIND has also written about CENTS: "Mass-produced objects become unique through circulation. Meaning doesn't come from rarity alone. It emerges from time, wear, and accumulated human use. Value is not invented. It is lived into existence."
And: "Bitcoin was the defining conceptual artwork of the networked era, authored by cypherpunks, executed in code. CENTS answers it materially, fixing that abstraction into history."
