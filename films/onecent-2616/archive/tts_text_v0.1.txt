Cent number twenty-six sixteen. A penny struck in nineteen twelve, at Philadelphia.
It is the only Cent from nineteen twelve in the collection.
Its surface has turned red. Full Red. In the Traits Atlas, full means red is the dominant surface condition, continuous across the coin, a clearly red cent at first glance.
Across Lincoln's head and down to his shoulder, grey-green films. Significant Verdigris. Significant means verdigris is a major visual actor, with strong presence, but not yet fully dominant.
Seventy-one Cents are Full Red. This is the oldest of them. The next is from nineteen fifty-seven.
Compare Cent number ninety-five forty, from nineteen eleven, whose only trait is Notable Obsidian. The same design, one year apart, a different surface.
Scarcity written by survival.
Among the ten thousand Cents, the older the year, the fewer there are. Two hundred and eight are wheat cents, from nineteen fifty-eight or earlier. Twelve years appear only once.
In the words of Martin Lehnen, in his essay "Reflections on Mint Years":
"These CENTS are extraordinary anomalies with absolute scarcity: nineteen fifty-four, nineteen forty-nine, nineteen thirty-nine, nineteen thirty-five, nineteen thirty-four, nineteen thirty, nineteen twenty-nine, nineteen twenty-eight, nineteen twenty-six, nineteen nineteen, nineteen twelve, and nineteen eleven stand as singular relics."
Coin collecting turned over.
