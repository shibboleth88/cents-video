Cent number twenty-six sixteen. A penny struck in nineteen twelve, at Philadelphia. The only Cent from nineteen twelve in the collection.
Its surface has turned red. Full Red. In the Traits Atlas, full means red dominates the surface: a clearly red cent at first glance.
Across Lincoln's head and shoulder, grey-green films. Significant Verdigris. Significant means a major visual actor, but not yet fully dominant.
Seventy-one Cents are Full Red. This is the oldest. The next is from nineteen fifty-seven.
Compare Cent number ninety-five forty, from nineteen eleven, whose only trait is Notable Obsidian. One year apart, a different surface.
Scarcity written by survival.
Among the ten thousand Cents, the older the year, the fewer there are. Twelve years appear only once.
In the words of Martin Lehnen, in his essay "Reflections on Mint Years":
"These CENTS are extraordinary anomalies with absolute scarcity... nineteen twelve, and nineteen eleven stand as singular relics."
Coin collecting turned over.
In the words of collector Dagmar:
"Unlike traditional coin collectors, who prefer old coins in pristine condition, we degens value the symbolic decay of these fiat coins."
