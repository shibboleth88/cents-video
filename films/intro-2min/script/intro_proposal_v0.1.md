## Script (narration, 331 words written, about 343 spoken)

In 2017 Rutherford Chang said: "I'm going to start hoarding pennies." The penny, he said, is "the lowest common denominator, it's like junk, it's like nothing."

A copper cent struck before 1983 holds more than a cent's worth of copper. Hoarding them, he said, was like "buying 1.7 cents of copper for one cent and it's still worth one cent." The work's statement begins from such "discrepancies in value."

Chang took ten thousand copper cents out of circulation and photographed every coin. Coins made to be identical turned out to differ, each marked by its own wear, toning and color.

He had the coins melted and cast into one block, *CENTS Block Copper*. In March 2024 he inscribed the image of each obverse on Bitcoin, one image to one satoshi, the smallest unit of bitcoin, and in April the block's model, which fills a whole block of the chain: *CENTS Block 839969*.

The coin, its image, its inscription, the copper and the model: each is a cent in its own way.

Collectors preferred the damaged to the clean. In the words of one, phorkish: "Funny that in real life I like the shiny pennies but with CENTS I want the dirty old ones." A council of collectors reviewed and voted on all ten thousand Cents three times, and agreed on nineteen traits.

CENTS is a work of its medium. It shows what happens to money when its record passes from a state to a public network that anyone can read and no one runs. Hito Steyerl wrote in 2016: "the value is not in the product but in the network." Released at one satoshi each, the inscriptions moved each cent's value from a figure set by the state into an ongoing process, set and set again by the people who keep, read and exchange each Cent.

The work's statement ends: "its value can continue to be redefined through this digitally scarce set of 10,000 and the block that encapsulates their transformation."

## Why this shape

- **Opens on Chang's own words** about the penny, then the value discrepancy, so value is the frame from the first ten seconds.
- **Every form a cent** is the middle: coin, image, inscription, copper, model.
- **The public, then the medium and the network, then the ongoing process**, the three points you asked the TL;DR to carry.
- **Ends on the work's statement**, "its value can continue to be redefined": no closing on the artist's death or the end of the penny. Both are left out.
- **Order and intent.** The melt is stated before the inscriptions, because the coins had been melted by the time of the release (ledger 03, R20). Nothing says why Chang did anything.
