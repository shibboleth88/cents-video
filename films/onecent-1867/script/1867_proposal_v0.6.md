## 2. Script v0.6

1. Cent #1867. *(play button, "look closer" sticker, one 3DCENTS turn)*
2. Circulation leaves traces. *(concept card)*
3. In the words of Emily Watlington, writing on Rutherford Chang in *Art in America*: "Circulation is another of Chang's key themes: how do things change as they move through the world?" *(lit quote card)*
4. **Cent #1867 keeps two records. The first is the penny's surface.**
5. A dark band of Significant Obsidian crosses the upper left, over the motto and Lincoln's head.
6. Green patches of Significant Verdigris gather on his cheek, behind his head and at his collar.
7. A third trait came from outside: Contaminated, Alien Secretion. In the Traits Atlas, Alien Secretion names "unidentifiable translucent or greenish films".
8. Compare it to Cent #4421, also dated 1964 and close to mint state. *(dissolve between the two)*
9. In March 2024, Chang inscribed the image of Cent #1867 on a satoshi, the smallest unit of bitcoin: a cent on a sat.
10. **The second record is Bitcoin's ledger. In the words of Satoshi Nakamoto, in Bitcoin's founding paper: "We define an electronic coin as a chain of digital signatures."** *(lit quote card)*
11. **This Cent's satoshi moved twice in the two days after the inscription, and once more on April 17, 2024. By November 2026, it had not moved again.** *(ledger plate)*
12. **The ledger carries on what the penny's surface began, transfer by transfer.**
13. **What this Cent is worth is decided again whenever someone looks at it, compares it, keeps it or lets it go.**
14. Cent #1867, a great Cent. *(closing card, one turn)*
15. Find out more about the project on cents.gallery, and explore the traits on cents.vision. *(end card with your two screenshots)*

**Length:** 260 spoken words, against 177 in v0.5. At eee's rate on earlier episodes, that is about **2:00 to 2:10** finished. If it should land under 2:00, the cut I'd make is "By November 2026, it had not moved again." (8 words). Line 13 (21 words) would save more, but it is the film's only line about value.

## 4. Sources and checks

- **Nakamoto.** I added the paper to the One Cent quote checker's corpus (`knowledge/sources/primary/nakamoto_2008.txt`, copied from `objecthood/sources/`). check_quotes.py reports FOUND. The control, with "records" in place of "signatures", reports MISSING.
- **Line 11.** Traced from `1867/ledger/LEDGER_1867.md`.
  - Transfers came 15 h 40 min, 1 d 7 h 19 min and 21 d 6 h 22 min after the reveal, so the first two fall within two days of it.
  - The third was on April 17, 2024 at 08:13 UTC, which is the same date in New York.
  - The satoshi was unspent when traced on November 25, 2026, which is why the line says "By November 2026".
- **Line 12.** My shortening of the art text's sentence (v0.6, "The medium"). I wrote "what the penny's surface began" instead of "wear and toning", because #1867's three traits are two patinas and a contamination, with no wear trait.
- **Line 13.** From the art text v0.5.1 clean ("What a Cent is worth is decided again whenever someone looks at one, compares it, keeps it or lets it go"), which is the guide for the videos. It is the project's own text, so it is spoken as narration, with no quote card. Art text v0.6 makes the same point as "an ongoing process that no central authority runs".

## 5. Relational notes

- **Referents.**
  - "The second record" (10) points back to line 4, about 40 seconds earlier. The word "record" is repeated so that the link holds.
  - "A third trait" (7) still counts traits: two are named as traits just before it.
  - "the inscription" (11) is the act in line 9. "it" (11) is the satoshi.
- **Order.**
  - The inscription (9) comes before the ledger (10). That says the inscription began the second record, which is true.
  - Nothing says Chang inscribed the image in order to make a record: there is no "so", "to" or "because".
  - Line 4 presents the two records as what the Cent keeps, not as what anyone meant.
- **Registers.**
  - "Record" works in three: the object (4), the medium (10 and 11), and how the two relate (12).
  - Value (13) is a fourth idea, not another way of saying the same thing.
- **Shape.**
  - The film opens on a question about circulation and answers it with two records.
  - The last idea before the name is value decided again. Then the end card points somewhere to look, so the film does not close as if the work were finished.
- **Superlatives.** "The smallest unit of bitcoin" is true of the satoshi, and it is there only to say what a satoshi is.
