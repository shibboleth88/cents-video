Cent number eighteen sixty-seven. A penny dated nineteen sixty-four, with no mint mark, generally indicating the Philadelphia mint.
Circulation leaves traces.
In the words of Emily Watlington, writing on Rutherford Chang in Art in America: "Circulation is another of Chang's key themes: how do things change as they move through the world?"
Rhea Myers, in her book Proof of Work, quotes the artist Barbara Kruger: "Money is an information-destroying medium."
A circulated copper cent is the exception. Three traits record where Cent number eighteen sixty-seven has been: two patinas and a contamination.
A dark band of Significant Obsidian crosses the upper left, over the motto and Lincoln's head.
Green patches of Significant Verdigris gather on his cheek, behind his head and at his collar.
Two hundred and twenty Cents carry Significant Obsidian, and three hundred and seventy-six Significant Verdigris. Twenty-nine carry both.
Both patinas grew from the copper. In the Traits Atlas, a patina "arises from internal chemistry".
The third trait came from outside: Contaminated, Alien Secretion. In the Atlas, contamination is "matter imposed upon copper", and Alien Secretion names "unidentifiable translucent or greenish films".
The Trait Council names such residues by reading the surface, without testing it. In the Atlas's words: "The subtrait names are therefore not claims of chemistry, but field names, tools for seeing."
Seventy-nine Cents carry Alien Secretion, and seventy of them carry verdigris too.
No other Cent has exactly this set of traits.
Cent number forty-four twenty-one is dated the same year, and is close to mint state. Its portrait is clean and bright.
In March twenty twenty-four, Chang inscribed the image of Cent number eighteen sixty-seven on a satoshi, and the physical coin was melted into CENTS Block Copper.
Released at one satoshi, the Cent's value moved from a figure set by the state into an ongoing process, set again each time someone reads it, keeps it or lets it go.
Cent number eighteen sixty-seven, a great Cent.
