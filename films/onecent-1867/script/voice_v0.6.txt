Cent number eighteen sixty-seven.
Circulation leaves traces.
In the words of Emily Watlington, writing on Rutherford Chang in Art in America: "Circulation is another of Chang's key themes: how do things change as they move through the world?"
Cent number eighteen sixty-seven keeps two records. The first is the penny's surface.
A dark band of Significant Obsidian crosses the upper left, over the motto and Lincoln's head.
Green patches of Significant Verdigris gather on his cheek, behind his head and at his collar.
A third trait came from outside: Contaminated, Alien Secretion. In the Traits Atlas, Alien Secretion names "unidentifiable translucent or greenish films".
Compare it to Cent number forty-four twenty-one, also dated nineteen sixty-four and close to mint state.
In March twenty twenty-four, Chang inscribed the image of Cent number eighteen sixty-seven on a satoshi, the smallest unit of bitcoin: a cent on a sat.
The second record is Bitcoin's ledger. In the words of Satoshi Nakamoto, in Bitcoin's founding paper: "We define an electronic coin as a chain of digital signatures."
This Cent's satoshi moved twice in the two days after the inscription, and once more on April seventeenth, twenty twenty-four. By November twenty twenty-six, it had not moved again.
The ledger carries on what the penny's surface began, transfer by transfer.
What this Cent is worth is decided again whenever someone looks at it, compares it, keeps it or lets it go.
