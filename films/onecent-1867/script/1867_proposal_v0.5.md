## 3. The closing (lines 8 and 9)

**In place (option A).**
- Line 8 states the act and what a satoshi is. "A cent on a sat" is the art text's own phrase. The presentation rules say to lead the crypto case with CENTS as a quintessence of its medium.
- Line 9 is a published source:
  - Martin Lehnen, "CENTS as an artistic expression of Bitcoin's core ideas" (Medium, 27 Mar 2025), concept C04.
  - check_quotes.py: FOUND. Control: with "government" in place of "state" it reports MISSING.
  - The article's title says what you asked for, so the line names it and the quote follows.
- It describes what CENTS does, never why Chang made it.

**Option B (narration only, no card), from the art text's essential sentence:**
> In March 2024, Chang inscribed the image of Cent #1867 on a satoshi, the smallest unit of bitcoin: a cent on a sat. On a network no state runs, its value became an ongoing process, set again each time someone reads it, keeps it or lets it go.

B keeps the "ongoing process" idea, which fits the rule that CENTS has no ending. A says the Bitcoin link outright. Either way the end card leaves the viewer with somewhere to go next, so the film does not close as if the work were finished.

- **A caution I kept:** the objecthood findings warn against making a theme of the cent and the satoshi both being smallest units. So "the smallest unit of bitcoin" is there only to say what a satoshi is. There is no "smallest unit of the dollar" parallel.
