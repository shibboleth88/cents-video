## 2. Line 12, option B (the art text)

> The traits are an offering: a reading of the coins proposed in public. Each such reading, a trait, a set a collector assembles, matters as far as people take it up, and the meanings of the work form in a continual coagulation of consensus.

- **Source.** Art text v0.5.1 ("The public"; also on the web intro), shortened. The original reads: "The traits are an offering: a reading of the coins proposed in public, with no authority beyond the use others make of it. Each such reading, a trait, a set a collector assembles, a curator's choice, matters as far as people take up its words and act on it, and the meanings of the work form in that continual coagulation of consensus." Its revision note says the phrase comes from the framing for Schwartzman&.
- **No quote card.** This is the project's own text, not a published source. Your rule for these texts is that published books and articles carry the quoted conceptual points, so in a film it is narration, said directly.
- **Fit.** It names "a set a collector assembles", so the Sets passage (13) follows from it without a bridge. It also replaces my bridge line from option A.
- **A published card to go with it, if you want one.** The art text's next sentence quotes PWR Studio in *Artists Re:Thinking the Blockchain* (2017): "a constant process of negotiating consensus, block by block". It is verified in the presentations book cards (p. 131), not in the One Cent quote checker's corpus, so it would need adding there first.
