Cent #1867.
Every physical coin joins two things: metal and stamp.
In a copper cent, the coin's face and its metal had come apart. Hoarding such coins, Chang said in 2017, was like "buying 1.7 cents of copper for one cent and it's still worth one cent."
Chang took the two apart. He inscribed the image of each obverse on Bitcoin, and had the physical coins melted and cast into one block, CENTS Block Copper.
Boris Groys describes two ways of archiving: "the object without an aura" and "the aura without an object." CENTS keeps both.
In the image of Cent #1867, the copper's own chemistry spreads over the stamp of the state.
A dark band of Significant Obsidian crosses the upper left, over the motto and Lincoln's head.
Green patches of Significant Verdigris gather on his cheek, behind his head and at his collar.
A third trait came from outside: Contaminated, Alien Secretion. In the Traits Atlas, Alien Secretion names "unidentifiable translucent or greenish films".
Compare it to Cent #4421, also dated 1964 and close to mint state.
A cent held at one cent by the state became a Cent whose value and meaning are set, and set again, by the people who keep, read and exchange it.
Cent #1867, a great Cent.
