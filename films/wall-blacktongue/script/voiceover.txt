This is the Wall of blacktongue.
Its signature Cent, number 5185, was struck in 1968, and carries a full desert patina and a ghost.
The Wall holds twenty-four Cents, from 1938 to 1980.
Among them is one of only two Cents from 1938.
And one of nine with a full gold patina.
That gold, with five other Cents, completes a Full Spectrum Set: a full verdigris, a full obsidian, a full red, a full desert, a full gold and an orange.
Only nine Full Spectrum Sets can exist, as there are only nine Full Gold Cents.
On 24 October 2026, two Walls held one: this Wall, and the wallet of the artist, Rutherford Chang.
Across its Cents, the Wall also covers four of the twelve contamination types, which makes a Contamination Set.
The traits were named by collectors, over two years of voting, and the Sets were identified from that catalog.
Every Wall on cents.vision is a record that keeps changing. This was blacktongue's, on 24 October 2026.
