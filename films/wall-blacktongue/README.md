# Collector Wall, episode 1: blacktongue (pilot)

The first episode of the Collector Wall series. Brief:
https://claude.ai/artifact/CjXo59mHFePDgPJnmkXZoe (the format, selection rules and care rules).

- `data/wall.json` is a snapshot of the Wall taken on 24 Oct 2026. It is built from
  `/mnt/project-files/collectors/`, and the wallet address is left out on purpose.
  Re-fetch it before the script locks, because Diamond Hands and the claims list are
  regenerated daily.
- `script/voiceover.txt` is draft 1: 168 words, which is about 67 s of speech at the
  eo pace measured on Sets film A (362 words in 145.3 s).

Every figure in the script was checked against the replay of all 10,000 Cents on the
page (`/mnt/project-files/collectors/raw/merged-new2.json`) and against `set-counts.php`
(generated 2026-10-24T14:39:05Z):
- 1938 has 2 Cents.
- 9 Cents have full gold.
- fullSpectrum = 2.
- The Wall covers 4 contamination types.
