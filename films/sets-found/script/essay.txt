## Sets (`/sets.html`)
The Trait Council has defined a number of **Sets** — **emergent structures identified within the collection's internal dynamics** (describe them this way, not as "collecting goals"). Each has a requirement and example qualifying Cents; the **Collectors** page shows the **Sets leaderboards** (who holds / is closest to each). The 4 **base patinas** are Verdigris, Obsidian, Desert, Red; the 2 **noble patinas** are Gold and Orange.

- **Patina Set** — at least one **Notable+** of each of the 4 base patinas. Examples: #412, #4679, #215, #3398.
- **Sig Set** — at least one **Significant+** of all 4 base patinas. Examples: #2956, #6059, #8353, #337.
- **Spectrum Set** — **Notable+** of all 4 base patinas *plus* Gold and Orange. Examples: #6078, #2568, #4554, #6223, #4788, #7799.
- **Fulls Set** — one **Full** of each of the 4 base patinas. Examples: #3981, #7823, #9533, #1771.
- **Full Spectrum Set** — one **Full** of all 4 base patinas *plus* Full Gold and Orange. Examples: #9786, #3861, #1079, #8185, #4273, #2785.
- **Contamination Sets** — the only Sets built on **contamination** rather than patina, scored by how many of the **12 contamination types** a collection covers across its Cents (Glue, Scotch Tape, 3D-black, Alien Secretion, Paint, Obscure Adhesive, Dark Grime, Sea Salt, Blood, Speckles, Unknown Liquid, Special Oddities):
  - **Contamination Set** — covers at least **3** of the 12.
  - **Contamination Hoard** — covers at least **6** of the 12.
  - **Full Contamination** — covers **all 12**.
- **Chronicle Set** — holds at least one Cent minted in **each of 1971, 1979 and 1982**.
- **Full Mint Set** — holds at least one **S-mint**, one **D-mint**, and one Cent with **no mint
  mark**. The S-mint is the hard part: the collection contains **60** S cents against 1,597 D
  and 8,343 with no mark, so this is one of the scarcer Sets despite sounding simple.
- **Decades Sets (1940s–1980s)** — hold at least one Cent from **every mint year of that decade present in the collection** (a 1940s, 1950s, 1960s, 1970s, and 1980s Set; the collection's years don't form full decades before the 1940s).
- **Three Decades Set** — a complete decade set for the **1960s, 1970s and 1980s** (1960–1982).
- **Full Decades Set** — a complete decade set for **every** decade present (all five, 1940s–1980s).
- **All Decades** — at least one Cent from **every decade present in the collection**, any mint
  year. "Every decade present" means **all eight** — 1910s, 1920s, 1930s and 1940s–1980s — not
  just the five that form complete decade Sets. It asks for one Cent per decade instead of
  every year, but it reaches back into decades the other Decades Sets ignore, and the
  collection holds only 5 cents from the 1910s, 9 from the 1920s and 12 from the 1930s. So do
  **not** describe it as the easy one: it is weaker per decade and much harder in reach.

> ⚠️ **There is no Corroded Set.** Corroded is a trait, and on the Lab it sits
> beside the Contaminated dropdown, so it is easy to assume a Set exists for it.
> Checked against `/sets.html` on 4 Sep 2026: the word doesn't appear there. If
> someone asks about "the Corroded sets," they most likely mean the
> **Contamination Sets** above.
>
> ⚠️ **It is the "Full Mint Set", not a "Mint Mark Set".** The Set is about holding all
> three mint marks; "Mint Mark Set" is a natural way to say that and is not what the site
> calls it.


The glossary entry (cents-bot knowledge/glossary.md), verbatim:

**Sets** — Emergent structures identified within the collection's internal dynamics by the Trait Council, tracked on cents.vision. Three families plus two standalones: the **patina** Sets (Patina, Sig, Spectrum, Fulls, Full Spectrum), the **Contamination** Sets (Contamination Set, Contamination Hoard, Full Contamination), the **Decades** Sets (one per decade 1940s–1980s, Three Decades, Full Decades, All Decades), and the **Chronicle** and **Full Mint** Sets. (Refer to them this way — not as "collecting goals.") There is no Corroded Set, and the mint-mark one is the *Full Mint* Set. See cents-vision-guide.md for each requirement and examples.

Of the 10,000, 15 are full verdigris, 38 full obsidian, 21 full desert and 71 full red.
Notable or more: verdigris 1,884, obsidian 1,757, desert 598, red 670. Gold 69, orange 141.
Exactly 9 Cents are full gold.
Contamination is recorded in 12 types. By count: Obscure Adhesive 150, Paint 145, Dark Grime 127, Unknown Liquid 109, Speckles 95, Alien Secretion 79, Glue 51, Sea Salt 49, Special Oddities 44, 3D-black 35, Blood 17, Scotch Tape 7.
Mint marks: no mark 8,343, D 1,597, S 60.
Mint years: 1971 has 362 Cents, 1979 has 640, 1982 has 867.
1949 has exactly one Cent. 1954 has exactly one Cent. They are the only years from 1940 to 1959 with a single Cent; 1940 and 1942 have two each.
Because a Decades Set requires every mint year of the decade present in the collection, the 1940s Set requires the one 1949 Cent and the 1950s Set requires the one 1954 Cent, so each can be held by at most one collector at a time. Full Decades requires both.
Caps on complete Sets held at any one time, each set by its narrowest ingredient: Fulls 15 (full verdigris), Full Spectrum 9 (full gold), Full Contamination 7 (Scotch Tape).
