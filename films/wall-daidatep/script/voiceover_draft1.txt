This is the Wall of Daidatep.
Its signature Cent, number 9908, was struck in 1956, carries a full desert patina, is Faceless, and is contaminated with Special Oddities.
The Wall holds 132 Cents, from 1944 to 1982, which puts it in the Dollar Club, for a hundred Cents or more.
Ten of them are wheat pennies, minted 1958 or earlier. The earliest is from 1944.
It holds four Cents from 1971 and four from 1969, for the 1971 Club and the 1969 Club.
Cent number 3640 is the only Cent from 1955 with a D mint mark, which makes it a 1/1 Mint.
Four of its Cents are 1/1 Trait Combos: each carries a pair of traits found on no other Cent. The signature Cent is one of them.
Seven of its Cents carry a full patina: four full red, two full obsidian and the full desert.
The Wall completes five Sets.
A Spectrum Set: all four base patinas, plus gold and orange.
A Chronicle Set: a Cent from 1971, from 1979 and from 1982.
A Full Mint Set: an S mint, a D mint and a Cent with no mint mark. Its S mint is from 1945.
A Three Decades Set: every mint year of the 1960s, the 1970s and the 1980s.
And a Full Contamination Set: all twelve contamination types.
As of October 2026, Daidatep is one of only two collectors to hold one of these rare Full Contamination Sets.
The Wall carries Diamond Hands: it has held its position for at least a year, with little to no selling.
And it is a Mint Keeper: Daidatep minted Cent number 845 and still holds it, never sold.
