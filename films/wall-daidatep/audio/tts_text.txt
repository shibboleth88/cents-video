This is the Wall of Daidatep.
Its signature Cent, number ninety-nine oh eight, was struck in nineteen fifty-six, carries a full desert patina, is Faceless, and is contaminated with Special Oddities.
The Wall holds a hundred and thirty-two Cents, from nineteen forty-four to nineteen eighty-two, which puts it in the Dollar Club, for a hundred Cents or more.
Ten of them are wheat pennies, minted nineteen fifty-eight or earlier. The earliest is from nineteen forty-four.
It holds four Cents from nineteen seventy-one and four from nineteen sixty-nine, for the nineteen seventy-one Club and the nineteen sixty-nine Club.
Cent number thirty-six forty is the only Cent from nineteen fifty-five with a D mint mark, which makes it a one of one Mint.
Four of its Cents are one of one Trait Combos: each carries a pair of traits found on no other Cent. The signature Cent is one of them.
Seven of its Cents carry a full patina: four full red, two full obsidian and the full desert.
The Wall completes five Sets.
A Spectrum Set: all four base patinas, plus gold and orange.
A Chronicle Set: a Cent from nineteen seventy-one, from nineteen seventy-nine and from nineteen eighty-two.
A Full Mint Set: an S mint, a D mint and a Cent with no mint mark. Its S mint is from nineteen forty-five.
A Three Decades Set: every mint year of the nineteen sixties, the nineteen seventies and the nineteen eighties.
And a Full Contamination Set: all twelve contamination types.
As of October twenty twenty-six, Daidatep is one of only two collectors to hold one of these rare Full Contamination Sets.
The Wall carries Diamond Hands: it has held its position for at least a year, with little to no selling.
And it is a Mint Keeper: Daidatep minted Cent number eight forty-five and still holds it, never sold.
