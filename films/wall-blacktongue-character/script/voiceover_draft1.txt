This is the Wall of blacktongue: twenty-five Cents, holding every grade of patina recorded in CENTS.
Verdigris, obsidian, desert and red, each at notable, significant and full; and gold, at notable and full.
Notable means clearly visible but local, significant a major presence on the face, and full the dominant surface.
No other collector's Wall holds all fourteen. CL7 and Daidatep, with more than a hundred Cents each, reach twelve. The only other Wall that holds them all is the artist wallet, with a thousand Cents.
The Wall also holds orange and corroded, so of the eight patinas, only iridescent is missing.
Of all the Walls with ten or more Cents, none carries more traits per Cent: two point four on average. Across CENTS, the average is under one.
Number 3217, from 1970, carries all four base patinas at once.
The wear runs the whole range, from close to mint state, as on number 2489, to three Faceless Cents, where Lincoln's face is all but gone.
No Wall with fewer Cents holds both ends.
Fourteen of the twenty-five are from the 1960s, against fewer than one in five across CENTS. Six are from 1968, including the signature Cent.
In a 2024 interview, blacktongue described the challenge as "finding additional value in the artistic merit and time-worn aesthetic of each coin," and spoke of "being patient and trusting my eye (and gut)."
Of number 5755, collected in August 2026, blacktongue wrote: "Discoloration and dark deposits have created an accidental work of art."
The newest Cent is number 7476, from 1976. It carries a significant verdigris patina, a notable obsidian patina, and obscure adhesive, thick, cloudy deposits.
It brings the Wall to twenty-five Cents, from 1938 to 1980, which puts it in the Quarter Club, for twenty-five to forty-nine Cents.
Its signature Cent, number 5185, was struck in 1968, carries a full desert patina, and is a Ghost Cent.
That full desert, with five other Cents, completes a Full Spectrum Set: a full verdigris, a full obsidian, a full red, a full gold and an orange.
Only nine Full Spectrum Sets can exist, as there are only nine Full Gold Cents. As of November 2026, blacktongue is still the only collector to hold one.
The oldest Cent is one of only two from 1938. With a Cent from 1958, it is one of two wheat pennies on the Wall.
Across its Cents, the Wall covers four of the twelve contamination types, which makes a Contamination Set.
It holds four of the sixty-nine Corroded Cents, and only two other collectors hold as many. Two of its Cents are Ghost Cents, and one is Vandalized.
And the Wall carries Diamond Hands: it has held its position for at least a year, with little to no selling.
